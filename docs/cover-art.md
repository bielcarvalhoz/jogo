# Arte da capa

Arte final: `public/art/city-of-god-cover.webp` (atlas 3 × 2).
Gerada com a ferramenta integrada de imagens, usando a capa anterior como
referência de composição e o print do jogo como referência dos personagens e cenário.
O atlas foi convertido para WebP, qualidade 88. Os recortes irregulares e diagonais
são feitos pelas seis máscaras CSS em `src/ui/style.css`, com faixas pretas mais grossas;
o grid do atlas não aparece como grid na capa.

## Prompt final de produção

Reinterpret the existing six-scene game-cover atlas as artistic arcade/cartoon
illustrations, inspired by the actual low-poly game and its characters. Use bold
faceted shapes, expressive faces, rich colors, graphic shadows and hand-painted
detail. Keep an illustrated game-cover feel. The supporting gameplay screenshot
defines the blue shirt, blonde hair, black headphones, mustard trousers, white
office towers with dark window grids, streets, trees and campus environment.
Compose six independent square scenes in a 3-column, 2-row atlas: a campus office
tower; the blonde headphone-wearing character in blue; a red-and-white bus by the
campus entrance; a dark-haired banker with glasses; an aerial view of the campus;
and two banker characters against a warm sunset. No lettering, logos, UI or frame
inside the illustrations. The consuming page supplies its own diagonal polygon
crops, black separators and title. Preserve recognizable references from the
original cover while making their rendering more cartoon-like and game-like.
